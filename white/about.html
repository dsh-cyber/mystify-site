About Mystify

We break the story. Then we build what carries it.

Mystify is a media technology company. Our newsrooms break the story. Our technology, Mystify Studios and Mystify Publishing carry it to every platform and format, from news apps and video to television, podcasts and books. Every product we offer partners runs first in our own newsrooms.

Who we are

Fifteen brands. One machine behind them.

Mystify owns and operates 15 digital brands across celebrity, entertainment, true crime, news, lifestyle and sport, led by iconic household names RadarOnline and OK!, to Wonderwall, Knewz and Front Page Detectives.

Our stories are seen more than 362 million times a month, on our own sites and everywhere audiences read: MSN, Yahoo, NewsBreak, Facebook, SmartNews, Apple News and more.

The engine is ours. Mystify AI reads every distribution platform in real time and tells editors what to publish, who should write it and how to headline it. Mystify Velocity puts that same operating model in partner newsrooms. VAUNT, our AI video platform, turns published articles into licensed, sourced video in every ratio, within two hours.

Then we take the story further.

Mystify Studios turns our journalism into non-scripted television, documentaries and podcasts for Investigation Discovery, TLC and REELZ, and is developing factual vertical microdramas drawn from the archive and made with AI. Mystify Publishing releases investigative non-fiction under Front Page Detectives with Skyhorse Publishing, plus three children’s biography series.

Our values

What we believe.

Five principles that shape how we report, build and work together.

  1. 01

    Break the story

    We set the agenda. The stories we tell, the products we build and the partnerships we make should change the conversation, for our audiences, our partners and our people.

  2. 02

    Own it

    We trust each other with real responsibility and hold ourselves to it. We decide quickly, stand behind our reporting and keep the standard high.

  3. 03

    Build what’s next

    We don’t wait for the industry to move. We test, ship and rebuild, and we put every new tool to work in our own newsrooms first.

  4. 04

    Every voice in the room

    Our audiences are diverse, and so are we. We value different perspectives, back every voice on our teams and reflect the communities we cover.

  5. 05

    Enjoy the chase

    We love the scoop, the launch and the win. We celebrate together, look after each other and make the work worth doing.

Our history

Built on breaking news.

Decades of award-winning journalism, and the brands, programming and technology built on it.

  1. 1936

    Front Page Detectives launches as an American true-crime pulp magazine. It runs until September 1995.

  2. 2003

    Radar magazine debuts in September.

  3. 2005

    American Media buys Radar and relaunches it as RadarOnline, which becomes the award-winning leader in breaking celebrity news and investigative entertainment journalism.

  4. 2005

    OK!, Britain’s royal and celebrity weekly, launches in the U.S. under Northern & Shell.

  5. 2009

    Microsoft launches Wonderwall to program MSN’s celebrity and pop-culture content “in a dynamic and visually arresting way.”

  6. 2011

    Dylan Howard, then senior executive editor of RadarOnline, is named Entertainment Journalist of the Year by the Los Angeles Press Club. The judges wrote that “even mainstream media couldn’t ignore exclusive stories broken under Dylan Howard’s tenure.”

  7. 2011

    Radar wins Los Angeles Press Club awards for the Mel Gibson audiotapes and for exposing a secret Hollywood poker ring involving Tobey Maguire, Ben Affleck, Matt Damon and Leonardo DiCaprio. Radar now holds 24 journalism awards.

  8. 2011

    American Media buys OK! USA.

  9. 2015

    Chattr launches as the home of Australia’s reality TV audience.

  10. 2016

    JonBenét: An American Murder Mystery becomes the most-watched series in Discovery Communications history, averaging 2.6 million viewers across its three-night premiere (L+3).

  11. 2017

    Casey Anthony: An American Murder Mystery draws 2.9 million viewers (Live+SD), the best premiere and series debut in Investigation Discovery history.

  12. 2018

    Fatal Voyage: The Mysterious Death of Natalie Wood becomes America’s No. 1 true crime podcast.

  13. 2019

    Fatal Voyage is named a Webby Honoree for Best Series.

  14. 2020

    News Corp launches Knewz in January.

  15. 2020

    EPSTEIN: Devil in the Darkness is named a Webby Honoree for Documentary.

  16. 2021

    In March, Dylan Howard brings Radar and OK! together under his new, privately owned media company. The deal returns Radar to the editor who took it to record traffic and awards, and reunites him with OK!, which he ran as Chief Content Officer at its previous publisher.

  17. 2021

    Front Page Detectives relaunches.

  18. 2021

    Morning Honey launches with practical daily help and positive inspiration for women.

  19. 2021

    News Corp sells Knewz to a Mystify subsidiary in August.

  20. 2022

    A Mystify subsidiary acquires Women’s Golf Journal.

  21. 2024

    A Mystify subsidiary acquires Wonderwall.

  22. 2025

    Mystify launches as the rebranded home of every title.

  23. 2026

    Mystify acquires Chattr.

  24. Today

    Mystify runs its brands, Studios and Publishing on its own technology, Mystify AI and Mystify Velocity. It’s launching VAUNT, with factual microdramas in development.

Leadership

Led by journalists.

Chief Executive Officer Dylan Howard is a twelve-time National Entertainment Journalism Awards winner and 14-time finalist, and a two-time Webby Awards Honoree for the true crime podcast series he executive produced.

The Los Angeles Times said Howard “transform(ed) America’s tabloid culture.” He is the author of seven books and has developed programming for Investigation Discovery, TLC and REELZ.
