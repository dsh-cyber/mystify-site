Mystify Publishing

Books that start in the newsroom.

Mystify Publishing turns the reporting behind our podcasts and television into book-length investigations under Front Page Detectives, with Skyhorse Publishing. For young readers, three biography series are published directly on Amazon.

The imprint

In 2019, Dylan Howard signed an exclusive deal to publish his true-crime and investigative titles with Skyhorse Publishing and Start Publishing, launching the Front Page Detectives imprint.

The list takes the reporting behind our television and podcast franchises, from Diana and Epstein to Natalie Wood and JonBenét, and develops it into book-length investigations published digital first in eBook and audio, alongside hardcover and paperback.

Children’s imprints

Mystify Publishing owns three children’s imprints, My Football Hero, My Sporting Hero and Heroes of History, published directly on Amazon.

Each book tells the life story of a real-life hero, from Cristiano Ronaldo and Michael Jordan to Neil Armstrong and Ruby Bridges, written for young readers so reading becomes something they choose.

Submissions

We accept manuscripts for review.

Mystify Publishing is looking for investigative non-fiction: true crime, celebrity, power and the stories behind the headlines.

Send a query letter, a one-page synopsis, the first three chapters or a full proposal, and a short author bio. Agented and unagented submissions are welcome. We read every manuscript and will be in touch if it is a fit for the list.

  • Query letter and synopsis
  • First three chapters or full proposal
  • Author bio and relevant reporting
