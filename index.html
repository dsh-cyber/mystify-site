How it works

From story to scale.

A great story is half the job. Our technology puts it in front of the readers who want it, on the platforms where they already read. Scale without relevance is noise.

  1. 01

    Journalists write.

    Mystify AI never writes our journalism. It gives editors pre-approved tools for headlines, section heads, outlines and captions.

  2. 02

    Data assigns.

    Real-time Rankings and Headline Lab show what is moving on each platform. The Virtual Assignment Desk hands it to the right writer within minutes.

  3. 03

    Platforms deliver.

    Stories go out to MSN, Yahoo, NewsBreak and social feeds, and every result shapes the next assignment.
